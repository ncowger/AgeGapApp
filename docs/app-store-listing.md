## Description

```
Birthday Gap keeps the birthdays, ages, and relationships of your family and friends on your iPhone and iPad. There is no account. Everything stays on the device.

People
Add a name, birthday, photo, and notes. Mark one person as you. Link a spouse, father, and mother, including parents from different households.

Birthdays
See who is celebrating next, including who has a birthday today. Reminders can arrive on the day or up to a year ahead, at 9 AM. A monthly summary can list everyone with a birthday that month.

Timeline
Scroll a life in birth order. Gaps between people stay visible, and you can tap any two people to see the exact difference in years, months, and days.

Age gaps
Compare everyone with you, or compare every pair. Sort from the closest ages to the furthest.

Family tree
The tree builds itself from the links you set. Spouses sit side by side. Pinch to zoom, drag to move around, and export a sharp picture or PDF.

Contacts
Import people who already have a birthday in Contacts, including their photo.

Privacy
Names, birthdays, photos, notes, and relationships remain on your device. Birthday Gap does not track you.
```

## App Review notes

```
This app requests full Contacts access so a person can import birthdays and contact photos in one step. Names, birthdays, photos, and relationships stay on the device. There is no account and no demo login.
```
