Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

Short version: Birthday Gap stores all your data only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Ever.

What Data We Collect

Birthday Gap does not collect any personal data. The app does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or any form of telemetry.

The following information is entered by you and stored exclusively on your device:

  • Names and birthdays you add manually
  • Profile photos you choose
  • Relationship links (spouse, parent) you define
  • Notes you write
  • Notification preferences

None of this data is transmitted off your device or accessible to us in any way.

Contacts Permission

If you choose to import contacts, the app requests read access to your Contacts. This access is used solely to display your contacts within the app so you can select which ones to import. Contact data is never uploaded, stored externally, or shared with any third party.

Photo Library Permission

If you choose to add a profile photo from your photo library, the app requests access to select a single photo. The selected image is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted anywhere.

Notifications

If you enable birthday reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are processed entirely on-device by iOS and do not involve any external server or third party.

iCloud Sync (Future Feature)

A future version of Birthday Gap may offer optional iCloud sync so your data stays in sync across your own Apple devices. If enabled, data would be stored in your personal iCloud account and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. This feature will always be opt-in. We will never have access to your iCloud data.

Third-Party Services

Birthday Gap does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash reporters. There are no third parties that receive your data.

Children's Privacy

Birthday Gap does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app is safe to use by or about children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
